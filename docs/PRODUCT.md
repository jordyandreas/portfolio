# PRODUCT

## Overview

This portfolio is the central hub of Jordy's professional presence.

Rather than acting as an online resume, this website showcases engineering thinking, product mindset, technical execution, and real-world experience through carefully crafted case studies.

The goal is to help recruiters and engineering leaders quickly understand not only what I built, but how I think and solve problems.

---

# Goals

## Primary Goals

- Increase interview opportunities.
- Demonstrate senior-level frontend engineering skills.
- Showcase software architecture decisions.
- Present real-world engineering case studies.
- Build long-term personal branding.

## Secondary Goals

- Publish technical articles.
- Share open-source work.
- Create a foundation for future blogging.
- Support freelance opportunities.
